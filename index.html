Backstory

Growing up in a loving home, Apollo cared deeply for his family. However, his parents expected great things from their only child, placing considerable pressure on him.

When Apollo was 23, his parents died in a tragic accident. Left with himself and the expectations they had placed upon him, Apollo left home to pursue something grand.

He decided to become a hero for hire. Through training, Apollo learned to harness his emotions into raw energy, beginning with the Determination that drove him to make a name for himself and make his parents proud.
